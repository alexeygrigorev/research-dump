# Berlin meetup and workshop venues

**Research snapshot:** 10 September 2026  
**Location:** Berlin, preferably Mitte or Prenzlauer Berg  
**Priority:** Budget-friendly recurring events for up to approximately 50 people

> Archived from the preceding research conversation. This document preserves the venue comparisons, source links, assumptions and unresolved questions from that response; the sources were not independently re-verified during the save. Prices and terms are research notes, not binding quotes or availability checks. Confirm current pricing, applicable contract terms and availability directly before booking.

## Brief and comparison assumptions

Two recurring event formats are being considered:

- **Monthly meetup:** Up to 50 people, chairs in rows, projector; no other special equipment requested.
- **Paid workshops:** Similar headcount, but with tables/desks and usable working space for participants. Ticketed events must be permitted.

The meetup estimates assume **four hours of room access, including setup and clearing up**. Workshop comparisons assume a **daytime workshop**, with the exact access hours to be confirmed where the venue publishes a general hire rate rather than an explicit day duration. Food and drinks are excluded.

Key questions are cost, advance-booking lead time, cancellation and rescheduling flexibility, and whether individual dates in a monthly series can be cancelled separately.

## Executive recommendation

**Start with Meeet Mitte for the monthly meetup, and compare martas with GLS Campus for paid workshops.** The main trade-off is that the cheaper workshop rooms fit 50 people around **group tables**, but not necessarily at **forward-facing desks**. GLS has a confirmed larger classroom layout; Meeet offers a much shorter free-cancellation window. See [Meeet Pavillon][meeet-pavillon], [Meeet prices][meeet-prices], [martas rooms][martas-rooms] and [GLS Lounge][gls-lounge].

For a first meetup with uncertain attendance, Meeet is the preferred starting point. For a firm evening date, GLS Room 112 has a lower published evening price but much earlier cancellation exposure.

**Do not pay for 50 desk places for the meetup, and do not book a “50-person room” for a workshop without getting the table layout confirmed.**

### Meeet cost calculation

Four hours at €74/hour, plus €25 for the projector and €25–40 for rearranging the room, then 19% VAT:

- Lower estimate: `(4 × €74 + €25 + €25) × 1.19 = €411.74`.
- Upper estimate: `(4 × €74 + €25 + €40) × 1.19 = €429.59`.

Room and projector alone are approximately **€382 including VAT**, but do not budget on receiving a 50-chair setup without an additional charge. Source: [Meeet prices][meeet-prices].

### GLS seasonality and evening discount

GLS high season is **May–June and September–November**; the other months receive the lower listed rate. The evening discount is particularly useful for a monthly meetup. Do not assume it can be combined with other discounts. Source: [GLS event brochure][gls-brochure].

**Choice:** Meeet for a first meetup whose attendance is uncertain; GLS Room 112 when the date is firm and price matters more than cancellation flexibility.

### Important qualifications

**martas VAT:** The research found the room and projector prices but no clear statement establishing whether those particular prices include VAT. Treat **€500–550 as the published sum to verify**, not a guaranteed final invoice. Changes to the standard furniture setup may also attract charges. Source: [martas conference brochure][martas-brochure].

**Laptop workspace:** Ask martas for a floor plan showing **50 usable working places, power access and sightlines to the screen**. Seating six people at a table is not, by itself, confirmation of sufficient laptop workspace.

**Ticketed workshops:** Ask each venue to confirm explicitly that charging participants is permitted and whether commercial events carry different rental terms or fees. No venue-specific approval for the proposed workshop has been obtained.

### Lower-cost option for a smaller initial class

**juggleHUB in Prenzlauer Berg** is worth considering for an initial **20-person workshop**, but not as a verified 50-person solution. Its conference room accommodates 20 at tables and costs approximately **€233 for four hours or €403 for the published day rate, including VAT**. Projector provision and any fee need confirmation. Source: [juggleHUB event hire][jugglehub-events].

This offers an alternative to paying for 50 desk places before workshop demand is established.

## 3. Cancellation and rescheduling flexibility

**Meeet is the better identified 50-person option for keeping the commitment relatively short. GLS and martas expose the organiser to cancellation charges much further ahead.** Get the applicable version of the terms attached to the quote rather than relying solely on website summaries.

| Venue | Free-cancellation deadline | Charges after that |
|---|---|---|
| **Meeet Pavillon — standard customer** | **14 days before the event**. | **50%** when cancelling between 14 and seven days beforehand. The room table does not explicitly state the charge inside seven days; request confirmation rather than assuming late cancellation is flexible. |
| **GLS Room 112** | **Eight weeks before**. | **50%** at four–eight weeks; **75%** at two–four weeks; **90%** inside two weeks. |
| **GLS Lounge** | **12 weeks before**. | **50%** at six–12 weeks; **75%** at three–six weeks; **90%** inside three weeks. |
| **martas Leipzig / Wien** | **Obtain an explicit free-cancellation deadline in writing.** The terms require written consent for a free withdrawal. | Published schedule: **25%** at 55–48 days; **50%** at 47–11 days; **80%** from ten days before; **90%** on the event day or for a no-show. Do not infer that cancellation earlier than 55 days is automatically free. |
| **juggleHUB — non-member** | **Seven days before**. | **50%** at four–seven days; **100%** later. Members have a shorter free-cancellation deadline of four days. **Reconfirm these terms:** the linked document dates from 2020. |

Sources: [Meeet prices and cancellation terms][meeet-prices], [GLS booking terms][gls-terms], [martas conference terms][martas-terms], [juggleHUB terms][jugglehub-terms].

### GLS deposits and contract scope

The GLS cancellation percentages apply to the **contracted total**, not just room rent. The linked booking terms also require an **80% deposit six weeks ahead for Room 112 and ten weeks ahead for the Lounge**. The document is older; request confirmation that it governs the proposed booking. Source: [GLS booking terms][gls-terms].

### Recurring bookings and date changes

**Rescheduling should be a separate question from cancellation.** Ask for one free date change and cancellation rights for **each individual monthly event**. Avoid letting a recurring booking become an inflexible commitment to an entire year.

Clarify whether a postponed event avoids cancellation charges, whether there is a deadline for choosing the replacement date, and whether a series discount is lost when one date is cancelled. These are negotiation questions, not confirmed venue policies.

## 4. How far in advance to book

The research did not identify a general, guaranteed **“book at least X days ahead”** rule for these rooms. Without actual dates, a reliable availability lead time cannot be established.

**Planning recommendation — not a venue rule:** Start enquiries **six to eight weeks before the first meetup**, asking about the next three monthly dates at the same time. For GLS, enquire **three to four months ahead** when retaining a free-cancellation period is important; a booking made closer to the event may already fall into a chargeable cancellation window.

### Operational deadlines are not availability guarantees

- **Meeet:** Setup and clearing up must fit inside paid rental time. Evening and weekend access must be agreed; the Pavillon normally has a **four-hour minimum during the day**. Source: [Meeet Pavillon][meeet-pavillon].
- **martas:** Technical and catering requirements are due **at least 14 days ahead**, and a deposit of **up to 80% may be due 30 days before**. These are planning/payment deadlines, not proof that a room can be booked at that notice. Source: [martas conference terms][martas-terms].

For a new paid workshop, negotiate a temporary hold or choose a cancellation deadline that allows ticket sales to be assessed before committing most of the venue budget.

## 5. Budget-saving routes

### Meeet FairPay

Meeet offers **10–25% room discounts** for eligible small businesses, sole traders and nonprofits. **Contact Meeet directly:** enquiries originating through booking marketplaces are excluded from FairPay. Eligibility can also improve the large-room free-cancellation deadline to **seven days**, although equipment has separate terms. Source: [Meeet prices and FairPay][meeet-prices].

This is the first discount to investigate for a recurring series, not a discount to assume eligibility for.

### Happy Pigeons: donation or revenue-sharing model

Happy Pigeons in Prenzlauer Berg publishes a relevant alternative model: **public events that are free to attend can use donation-based pricing**, while **public ticketed events use a negotiated share of sales**. Private event hire starts at **€290/day**, and a projector and screen are available on request. Source: [Happy Pigeons space hire][happy-pigeons].

**This is a promising budget enquiry, not a confirmed 50-person recommendation.** The main room is **50 m²**, and the research did not verify a published seated or desk capacity. Its standard rental terms allow free cancellation until **four weeks ahead**; clarify how cancellation works for donation-based or revenue-share events. Source: [Happy Pigeons space hire][happy-pigeons].

## 6. Recommended next steps

For the **monthly meetup**, request a direct quote from **Meeet Pavillon**, including FairPay eligibility and self-service evening access. Compare it against **GLS Room 112’s evening offer**, but choose GLS only when comfortable with the earlier cancellation commitment.

For **50-person paid workshops**, request **martas Leipzig for Friday/weekend dates**, or **Wien for other dates**, provided group tables work for the teaching format. For 50 people at forward-facing desks in Prenzlauer Berg, use **GLS Lounge as the comparison quote**.

### Enquiry template

**Subject: Recurring meetup and ticketed workshop room hire — up to 50 people**

> I’m planning a recurring monthly meetup for up to 50 people, with four hours of room access including setup and clearing up, chairs in rows and a projector. Separately, I’m planning ticketed workshops requiring tables and usable working space for up to 50 participants.
>
> Please quote both formats, including VAT, equipment, furniture setup, cleaning and any evening/weekend charges. Please also confirm the available recurring dates, any advance-booking requirement, deposit, free-cancellation deadline, date-change policy, and whether individual dates in a series can be cancelled independently.
>
> For the workshop, please confirm that ticket sales are permitted and share a floor plan showing 50 working places, power access and visibility of the presentation screen. Please specify how many hours of access the quoted price includes, including setup and clearing up.

### Unresolved items to confirm before booking

- Actual availability, evening/weekend access, access duration and recurring-series discounts.
- Final all-in pricing: VAT, projector/screen, furniture arrangement, cleaning, staffing, minimum spend and any compulsory catering.
- Fifty-person workshop working layout, adequate power and Wi-Fi, and permission to run a ticketed event.
- The current applicable contract, exact cancellation boundaries, deposit refund treatment, date-change rights and independence of each recurring date.
- Meeet cancellation charges inside seven days; martas VAT and free-cancellation entitlement; current applicability of the older GLS and juggleHUB terms; Happy Pigeons seated capacity and event-specific cancellation model.

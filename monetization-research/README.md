## Read this first

The strongest default expansion is a **bounded Enterprise AI Shipping Sprint**. The clearest distinct new employer-side opportunity is **AI hiring-loop design and interviewer calibration**. Test one paid engagement in each before building a platform or adding another broad course.

For the community business, incremental **developer activation services** are attractive, but community revenue is not automatically Alexey's personal distributable income. Employer Labs plans and vendor academy licensing are logical recurring or reusable extensions after validation.

## Central recommendation

Sell employers and developer vendors a specific additional outcome: a tested workflow, a usable hiring process, a maintained onboarding asset or an independent research answer. Preserve useful free public education. Sell execution, accountability and organizational coordination rather than making existing access worse.

Alexey already publicly advertises paid education, consulting, private workshops, DevRel services, Labs and a USD 10,000 AI-Native Business Operating System offer. Their public prices do not prove sales, but a new opportunity must beat their **actual contribution per founder hour**. Do not mistake repackaging existing offers for discovering an entirely new business.

## Illustrative two-offer portfolio

All figures below are analyst assumptions, not forecasts, actual income or validated demand. EUR, ex VAT, before tax and ownership splits.

| Scenario | Paid engagements | Revenue | Cash costs | Founder hours | Cash surplus before founder pay | Economic surplus at EUR 150/hour |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Downside | 2 shipping sprints + 1 hiring package | 44,000 | 29,000 | 254 | 15,000 | -23,100 |
| Base, normalized prices | 6 shipping sprints + 6 hiring packages | 156,000 | 52,000 | 352 | 104,000 | 51,200 |
| Upside | 10 shipping sprints + 12 hiring packages | 276,000 | 76,000 | 460 | 200,000 | 131,000 |

**Pilot-pricing correction:** The normalized portfolio uses an EUR 18,000 average sprint price. With exactly two EUR 12,000 founding sprints plus four EUR 20,000 regular sprints, the base portfolio instead produces **EUR 152,000 revenue, EUR 100,000 cash surplus and EUR 47,200 economic surplus**. This correction from the conversation is intentionally preserved alongside the original normalized model.

For the normalized base case, economic surplus is EUR 77,600 at EUR 75/hour, EUR 51,200 at EUR 150/hour, and **negative EUR 1,600 at EUR 300/hour**. The displaced alternative breaks even around EUR 295 of contribution per founder hour.

## Important boundaries

- All 100 opportunities are **alternatives, not additive forecasts**. Delivery capacity, cannibalization and customer overlap matter.
- Public followers and members are overlapping channels, not unique paying prospects. Reach is not conversion evidence.
- Competitor presence establishes an adjacent category, not spare market capacity, achievable volume or willingness to pay for this particular offer.
- Potential moat is something to build, not something already owned. Original assessments, calibrated delivery partners, permissioned outcomes and employer relationships are stronger candidates than generic content.
- No private financial statements, paid membership analytics, contracts or ownership arrangements were available. No prospect was contacted.
- Keep uncommitted cash at risk below EUR 5,000 before paid validation. The proposed first-90-day founder budget is 104 hours.
- Preserve consent, contributor rights, open-source terms and editorial independence. Do not resell member lists, confidential employer material or copied job postings as proprietary data.
- Refresh prices, dates and relevant legal requirements before acting. The cited research is a dated snapshot, not a fresh verification at the time of any later repository visit.

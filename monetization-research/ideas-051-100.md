[Archive index](README.md) · [Ranks 1–50](ideas-001-050.md) · [Model inputs](model100.csv) · [Sources](sources.md)

Research snapshot: **7 September 2026**. All prices, volumes, costs, hours and scores are analyst hypotheses. EUR, ex VAT, before tax and ownership splits; economic surplus deducts founder time at EUR 150/hour. These are alternatives, not additive forecasts. A negative first-year cash-based result does not prove an idea has no long-term value. Scores are judgments, not probabilities. Future moat must be earned.

## Why the tail is still worth retaining

These are an option inventory, not recommendations to launch. Software can become attractive after paid service clients repeatedly ask for the same capability. Events and publishing may support relationships or acquisition despite weak direct first-year returns. Investing is a separate capital-allocation decision. Re-rank only when evidence changes: a real buyer, cheaper repeatable delivery, better retention, owned rights, or an independently verifiable distribution advantage—not merely excitement about a technical prototype.

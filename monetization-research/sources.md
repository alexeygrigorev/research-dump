# Source register and research limitations

Research snapshot: **7 September 2026**. This archive preserves the sources and qualifications from the original research; archiving is not a new verification of every page.

Public professional pages and official provider sources were prioritized. The Field Guide README was read through connected GitHub; only public repository content was used. No private financial statements or personal contact lists were obtained, and no prospect was contacted.

A source supports the adjacent fact identified below. **It does not validate proposed sales volumes, prices, returns, market capacity or comparative superiority.** Refresh prices, product terms, cohort dates and relevant legal requirements before acting. Replace public reach numbers with permissioned, deduplicated analytics and modeled costs with real contracts.

## What remains unverified

Actual sales, collected revenue, contribution per founder hour, active paid members, paid retention, employer-funded purchases, sponsor margins, available capacity, ownership and contributor agreements, willingness to pay for these specific offers, and customer outcomes. Public prices and audience figures do not answer these questions.

## Refresh procedure

Before launching, verify the chosen competitor and legal sources, retrieve permissioned business analytics, replace model assumptions with quotes and signed contracts, and record the date and reason for each change. Separate original research from subsequent experimental evidence. Require paid commitments and accepted deliverables before attributing market validation.

# People to Follow

A virtual advisory board for an AI-native, founder-led portfolio company spanning community, education, media, and software.

## Dan Shipper

**Core domain:** AI-native portfolio company; media + software

**Why relevant:** Closest structural analogue to your mix of publishing, education, software, and AI agents. Study how Every turns shared editorial and technical capabilities into multiple products.

**Best starting episode:** [The AI-native startup: 5 products, 7-figure revenue, 100% AI-written code](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/inside-every-dan-shipper)

**Virtual advisory role:** Portfolio architecture and AI-native operating model

## Claire Vo

**Core domain:** AI product leadership; personal AI operating systems

**Why relevant:** Combines a senior product role, a small AI product business, and unusually integrated agent workflows. Useful for founder command-center design and productizing internal tools.

**Best starting episode:** [OpenClaw changed my life: My AI operating system](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-openclaw-changed-my-life-claire-vo)

**Virtual advisory role:** Founder OS and productized agents

## Rob Walling

**Core domain:** Bootstrapped SaaS; portfolio building; small teams

**Why relevant:** Author of The SaaS Playbook and operator across podcast, conference, accelerator, books, and software. His work is the best bridge from your current book to a broader portfolio company.

**Best starting episode:** [Creator Science #294: Team building, owner-level thinkers, and vibe coding](https://podcast.creatorscience.com/rob-walling/)

**Virtual advisory role:** Bootstrapped company design and owner-level team building

## Jay Clouse

**Core domain:** Paid community; creator operations; founder-led marketing

**Why relevant:** Strong at connecting membership economics, content systems, audience trust, and a lean team. Directly applicable to AI Shipping Labs.

**Best starting episode:** [Startups for the Rest of Us 821: How to Do Founder-Led Marketing](https://www.startupsfortherestofus.com/episodes/episode-821-how-to-do-founder-led-marketing-with-jay-clouse)

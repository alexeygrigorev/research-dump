# Start-here listening sequence

### 1. Lenny's Podcast — [The AI-native startup: 5 products, 7-figure revenue, 100% AI-written code](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/inside-every-dan-shipper)

**Guest(s):** Dan Shipper  
**Published:** 2025-07-17  
**Topic:** Company Design

A concrete case of a founder operating a portfolio of media, software, and AI products with unusually high leverage. For Alexey, it maps directly to designing a small portfolio company where DataTalks.Club, AI Shipping Labs, courses, and software share one operating system instead of becoming four separate organizations.

**Experiment:** Draw one shared flywheel for DataTalks.Club, AI Shipping Labs, Buildcamp/courses, and internal tools; eliminate any offer that does not strengthen it.

### 2. AI & I (Every) — [We Gave Every Employee an AI Agent. Here's What Happened](https://every.to/podcast/transcript-we-gave-every-employee-an-ai-agent-here-s-what-happened)

**Guest(s):** Brandon Gell; Willie Williams  
**Published:** 2026-04-08  
**Topic:** AI & Agent Operations

It shows an operating model in which agents are visible collaborators, specialize over time, and can be coordinated by higher-level agents. For Alexey, it maps directly to turning your existing agent experiments into governed, repeatable workflows with clear inputs, outputs, permissions, and review gates.

**Experiment:** Choose one shared queue—content, member support, or course operations—and make every agent's plan, artifacts, and approvals visible there.

### 3. Creator Science — [#294: Rob Walling — Team building, owner-level thinkers, and vibe coding](https://podcast.creatorscience.com/rob-walling/)

**Guest(s):** Rob Walling  
**Published:** 2026-02-24  
**Topic:** Company Design

Walling's portfolio spans podcasting, conferences, investing, books, and software, so the coordination problem is much closer to yours than a pure SaaS case. For Alexey, it maps directly to designing a small portfolio company where DataTalks.Club, AI Shipping Labs, courses, and software share one operating system instead of becoming four separate organizations.

**Experiment:** List every brand and recurring output you run; pick the two umbrella brands that should own the rest and assign one accountable human or agent to each recurring result.

### 4. Startups for the Rest of Us — [Episode 661: Millions in Revenue as a One-Person Software Company](https://www.startupsfortherestofus.com/episodes/episode-661-millions-in-revenue-as-a-one-person-software-company)

**Guest(s):** Mike Perham  
**Published:** 2023-05-16  
**Topic:** SaaS, Product & Open Source

Sidekiq is a rare durable example of one person monetizing an open-source ecosystem without constructing a conventional organization. For Alexey, it maps directly to building small tools or products around real community workflows while preserving simplicity, trust, and open-source leverage.

**Experiment:** Identify one repeated technical pain in your courses or community that could support an open core, paid convenience layer, or support contract.

### 5. Creator Science — [#304: How Sam Vander Wielen built an $8M+ business around one product](https://podcast.creatorscience.com/sam-vander-wielen/)

**Guest(s):** Sam Vander Wielen  
**Published:** 2026-05-05  
**Topic:** Company Design

This is a strong non-software counterexample: a nearly one-product business with very little full-time headcount and disciplined operations. For Alexey, it maps directly to designing a small portfolio company where DataTalks.Club, AI Shipping Labs, courses, and software share one operating system instead of becoming four separate organizations.

### 6. The Tim Ferriss Show — [#694: The Ultimate Guide to Virtual Assistants, 10x Delegation, and Winning Without Talent](https://tim.blog/2023/09/20/sam-corcos/)

**Guest(s):** Sam Corcos  
**Published:** 2023-09-20  
**Topic:** Delegation & Team

Corcos treats delegation as a designed system of documentation, review, and trust rather than a vague request to 'take things off my plate'. For Alexey, it maps directly to deciding what stays with you, what belongs to Valeriia or specialist humans, and what can become an agent-run or contractor-run process.

**Experiment:** Keep a two-week delegation log: every repeated task gets a trigger, definition of done, example output, risk level, and proposed human/agent owner.

### 13. Creator Science — [#174: Bonnie Christine — Hiring and delegating in a creator business](https://podcast.creatorscience.com/bonnie-christine/)

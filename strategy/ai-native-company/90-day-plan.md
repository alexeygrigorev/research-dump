# 90-Day Implementation Plan

The objective is to install a small number of operating loops, not to maximize the number of AI tools or agents.

| Week | Theme | Listen | Build / experiment | Success signal |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Define the company you are building | Ranks 1 and 3 | Write a one-page portfolio thesis: free trust engine, paid transformation, software/tools, shared agent layer. | One portfolio map; no more than five active strategic bets |
| 2 | Observe founder bottlenecks | Ranks 6 and 13 | Run a two-week time and delegation log; classify every task as founder-only, approve, automate, delegate, or delete. | Baseline founder hours by category |
| 3 | Create the workflow inventory | Ranks 2 and 9 | Document 15 recurring workflows with trigger, source of truth, output, owner, risk tier, review gate, and KPI. | 15 workflows documented; risk levels assigned |
| 4 | Launch a chief-of-staff agent | Ranks 19 and 34 | Build a read-first founder command center and daily/weekly decision queue; keep all actions reversible. | Fewer context switches; 90% of commitments captured |
| 5 | Systemize the content engine | Ranks 11, 15, and 17 | Automate research, transcription, repurposing, and metadata while retaining Alexey's thesis and final editorial gate. | Founder production time down; no decline in quality score |
| 6 | Design community pathways | Ranks 7, 8, and 14 | Create three AI Shipping Labs pathways with milestones, peer rituals, shipped evidence, and targeted onboarding. | Activation and first milestone rates |
| 7 | Build voice-of-member intelligence | Rank 16 plus Indie Hackers 224 | Create a privacy-aware weekly synthesis of questions, obstacles, desired outcomes, and language with source links. | At least three decisions per month grounded in member evidence |
| 8 | Redesign paid learning value | Ranks 10 and 5 | Move value from information access toward feedback, accountability, environment, judgment, and proof of work. | Clear before/after promise; delivery capacity model |
| 9 | Simplify offers and pricing | Creator Science 271 and 277; SFTROU 271 | Draw the offer ladder, score every revenue stream, and remove overlapping or founder-heavy offers. | One obvious next step per product; margin and founder-hour visibility |
| 10 | Automate support safely | SFTROU 267 plus REWORK support episodes | Implement L0 self-service, L1 agent triage, L2 human operations, L3 founder escalation, and sampled QA. | Response time down; escalation quality; support themes drive fixes |
| 11 | Install engineering and data guardrails | How I AI Intercom plus Stripe agent-native episode | Use sandboxes, least privilege, tests, backups, dry runs, two-key approval, and audit logs for high-risk actions. | Restore drill passed; no autonomous destructive production access |
| 12 | Run the first AI-native quarterly review | Ranks 18 and 20 | Compare the new model with your own DataTalks.Club history; decide what to scale, stop, delegate, or keep founder-led. | Next-quarter priorities, owners, budgets, and kill criteria agreed |

## Weekly review questions

1. Which founder task repeated this week and should be deleted, delegated, or automated?
2. Which agent output required correction, and what reusable instruction, example, test, or permission boundary should change?
3. Which member or customer signal changed the product or curriculum thesis?
4. Which initiative is consuming coordination without strengthening the shared flywheel?
5. What is the one decision only Alexey can make next week?

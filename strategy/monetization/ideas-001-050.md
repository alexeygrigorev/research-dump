# All 100 opportunities: ranks 1–50

[Archive index](README.md) · [Ranks 51–100](ideas-051-100.md) · [Model inputs](model100.csv) · [Sources](sources.md)

Research snapshot: **7 September 2026**. Every proposed price, volume, cost, hour estimate and score is an analyst hypothesis, not validated sales. EUR, ex VAT and before tax/ownership splits. Economic surplus deducts founder time at EUR 150/hour. All ideas are standalone alternatives, not an additive forecast. The score is a prioritization judgment, not a probability. Scale and moat use 1–5; future moat must be earned.

## 1. Enterprise AI Shipping Sprint

**Score 89; scale 4; moat 3 → 4; Expand.** Buyer: CTO or head of data at a 50–500-person company with 5–12 participating engineers. Offer: three weeks on one approved use case, including baseline, reference implementation, evaluation gate, runbook and team handover.

**Economics:** EUR 18,000 average per sprint × 8; revenue 144,000; launch cash 4,000; all annual cash costs 46,000; founder time 236 hours; economic surplus 62,600; ROI 77%.

**Edge:** Production ML experience, existing course assets and a trusted route to technical champions. **Risk:** Custom delivery consumes founder time; security access and procurement delay kickoff. **Paid test:** Two signed pilots with deposits; at least 70% reusable material and no more than 20 founder delivery hours per pilot. First cash hypothesis: 3–8 weeks. Comparables: AI Makerspace, internal platform teams, specialist consultancies. Sources: S01;S05;S09;S13.

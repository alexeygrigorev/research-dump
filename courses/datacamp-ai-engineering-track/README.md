## Market basis (AI Engineering Field Guide, 9,760 jobs)

Every stage and technology choice in this track reflects what employers actually require in 9,760 AI Engineer job postings (Feb–Nov 2026):

- **Applied integrator role, not model research**: 97.4% of postings are applied/production (only 2.6% research). Within AI-First roles (68.9% of the market), the **integrator stack** (RAG, agents, APIs) rose from 80.0% in February to **91.7%** in November, while the trainer stack fell from 37.5% to 27.2%. Fine-tuning is a primary responsibility in only 3.2% of AI-First roles and absent from responsibilities in 86.0%.
- **Full-stack beyond GenAI (86.9%)**: Python is mandatory (71.6% overall, 75.9% AI-First), followed by **GenAI + Ops** (71.0% of AI-First: **CI/CD** 36.9%, **Docker** 24.2%, **Kubernetes** 23.6%), **GenAI + Web** (59.2%: APIs 2,515 jobs, REST APIs 1,630, **FastAPI** 928, React 1,373), **SQL** (14.6%, rising 11.1% → 14.9%), and cloud (**AWS** 39.3% overall / 41.8% AI-First > Azure 29.7% > GCP 29.1%).
- **RAG + Agents are the core**: **Agents (any)** appear in **56.2%** of all jobs (**74.0%** AI-First) and **RAG** in **40.1%** (**56.0%** AI-First). Jobs requiring *both* RAG and agents jumped from 33.1% to **51.0%** of AI-First postings.
- **Standard agent & retrieval stack**: **LangChain** (21.1% overall, 76.8% of framework adopters) and **LangGraph** (14.4% overall, climbing 7.4% → 15.2% and 37.7% → 71.0% among framework adopters) are the #1 co-occurring pair (3.5x lift). **MCP** (15.6%) is the fastest-rising named skill (9.9% → **20.4%**, +0.94 pp/scrape), followed by **Function Calling** (15.1%, 9.6% → **18.5%**). **Vector Databases** (2,201 jobs) and **PostgreSQL** (1,020 jobs; `pgvector`) lead data storage.
- **Evaluation is the differentiator**: **61.8%** of AI-First roles require evaluation skills (rising 55.9% → **69.3%** in November), and Evaluation & Testing appeared in **79.0%** of November responsibilities (+9.6 pp). LLM-native eval tooling (LangSmith, Langfuse, Guardrails, LLM Evaluation) rose 26.7% → **34.5%** while classic MLOps declined, and **Guardrails** rose 10.2% → **17.5%**.
- **Real use cases**: Automating manual workflows (**58.8%**), internal operational efficiency (**57.5%**), agentic systems (**49.9%**), finding information in company data (**43.8%**), and document processing (**13.3% → 16.0%**), while consumer-facing Q&A (-13.8 pp) declined.

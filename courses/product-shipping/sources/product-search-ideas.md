# Product-search ideas

Short notes on ways participants can find a product idea for Product Shipping with AI.

- https://x.com/scaling_shields/status/2126376257684390294, @scaling_shields (James Shields), Friday, 27 November 2026: scrape recent 1-star and 2-star Trustpilot and G2 reviews of competitors and email those reviewers. Useful as a product-search idea: public complaints show who already has the pain and may want an alternative.
- https://x.com/grok/status/2126557284159406429, @grok, Saturday, 28 November 2026, reply to @Denche2005 ("AI took your job today. You have $5,000 and 90 days. How do you build a new income, week by week?"): pick one service niche where AI still struggles (judgment, trust, local ops), get pilot clients, then productize the offer and later automate delivery or add a digital product. Useful as a product-search idea: start from a service you can sell now and turn it into a product.

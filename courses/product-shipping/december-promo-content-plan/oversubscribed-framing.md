# Oversubscribed framing — 20 seats

Principles are from Daniel Priestley's *Oversubscribed* as summarized in this repo's [action checklist](../../../learning/book-action-checklists/oversubscribed.md) (a summary-based edition, not the full text). The goal: build real demand for 20 seats, and say plainly that most interested people will not get one.

## The one claim every talk makes

> The first cohort has **20 seats**. Places are selected by application. The cohort starts **Monday, January 11, 2027**.

Use this only as true. Before any talk, confirm the points below.

| Check | Why | Status |

## Scenario A — an application round is open in December
- Every talk ends with the application link (https://forms.gle/F3ZiLzq8i2nip2VP6) and the deadline.
- Talk 4 may state the true application count against 20 seats.
- The deadline is the "reason to act now" (principle 4: a genuine reason, not invented urgency).

## Scenario B — the "Closes on 29 Nov" round is closed
- December talks serve three honest purposes: (1) a waitlist/interest list for any seat that opens or a next round, (2) direct enrollment ($3,000, if capacity allows), (3) building the audience for the next cohort.
- Do not describe the closed round as open, and do not use a fake countdown.
- The scarcity message becomes "the first cohort's selected places are closed / nearly full" **only if true**.

Alexey picks A or B; the plan's talks are unchanged, only the closing block differs.

## How each Oversubscribed principle shows up

| Principle (checklist) | In this plan |

## Wording rules

Use:
- "20 seats in the first cohort", "selected by application", "starts January 11".
- "I'm going to say no to most people who ask" is acceptable **only if true**.
- Show the standard: build regularly, test three peers' products, submit at midterm and final.

Avoid:
- Fake countdowns, fake "last seat", invented waitlist numbers.
- "Guaranteed customers/revenue/product-market fit" (FAQ says not).
- Calling lower-upfront options "scholarships" or "discounts"; the revenue-share terms are not finalized — direct people to the form.
- Saying "only 20 people can join" while direct enrollment is separate or uncapped.
- Comparing against AI Dev Tools Zoomcamp in a way that diminishes it (older pack's explicit guardrail).
- Using DataTalks.Club accounts as the course channel. (Credentials on the page: Alexey is "Founder of DataTalks.Club"; that is his bio, not a promotion channel.)

3. **Dec 17:** Show a review and who gets selected out. CTA: apply; deadline reminder (if real).
4. **Dec 22:** True demand status; what the cohort adds. CTA: apply / waitlist.
5. **Dec 29:** Last call; what happens next. CTA: final step.
6. **Between talks:** short clips, one real take-away per post, replies to questions. No selling-only posts.

## Measuring (do not conflate)

Track per person, strongest signal only: registered → attended/watched → replied/asked → applied → selected → paid. Report each separately. The checklist's 5×/10×/100× multiples are heuristics, not conversion rates.

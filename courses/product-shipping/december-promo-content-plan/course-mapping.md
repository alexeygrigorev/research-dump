## Cross-cutting verified elements to reuse in every talk's close

- Same product improved through the whole course.
- Group (not private) personal reviews of every submitted midterm and final project.
- Templates: product briefs, testing checklists, feedback templates, launch report template, review worksheets.
- Time: ~1 h live + ~4 h projects + ~1 h notes/recordings per teaching week; budget $20–$500/month for assistant and cloud, not included.
- No prior coding/Git/CLI required; ability to install and run an AI coding assistant is required.
- AI features in the product are optional.
- Maven Guarantee applies; no outcome guarantee.

## Content not covered by the verified syllabus (do not present as course content)

- Product defensibility / moats / AI-copying (requested topic; separate research intake; parked).
- Guest experts (none confirmed).
- Pricing/revenue-share percentages (not finalized).
- Certificates, lifetime access, refund specifics beyond "Maven Guarantee" (not verified).
- Testimonials or prior-cohort outcomes (none).

# Source register and evidence limits

**Consolidated 9 November 2026.** The school research was conducted on 7–8 November; the tax analysis and operational cross-checks were added on 9 November. This is a dated research snapshot, not a live monitor. Direct source links appear in the relevant files as well as here.

Use legislation and official guidance for requirements, provider pages for advertised offers, and employer postings as individual examples. Interpretations and proposed operating choices are labelled in the plan. Survey thresholds, founder-hour estimates, spending envelopes and cohort economics are planning assumptions, not findings from these sources.

## Initial distribution sequence

1. Publish one main post through the existing personal professional audience.
2. Share the same form through the Berlin Meetup/community channel.
3. Add a short block to an existing newsletter for readers in Germany.
4. Mention the study briefly at a relevant existing event, where appropriate for that event and audience.
5. Ask a small number of existing contacts to forward the invitation to relevant people.
6. Send one reminder after roughly a week, using an actual learning or unresolved question.

Use a consistent set of offer cards and one survey. Capture acquisition source with a link parameter or an optional question. Start with existing channels rather than adding a new regular event series or a paid advertising programme.

Do not request public replies identifying people who were laid off. Optional employment context belongs in the private research form. Contact people individually only within their stated permissions.

## January–March follow-up

If the research supports continuing, maintain contact with opted-in candidates through a modest cadence:

- One useful existing Labs event or demonstration each month, if access and the audience fit.
- A short factual progress update on the programme and approvals.
- Reconfirmation of April availability and the proposed daytime schedule.
- A concrete next step, such as a readiness exercise or an admissions conversation, when appropriate.

After the relevant approvals, provide accurate course facts for discussions with funding advisers. Do not imply that a planned or certified course automatically makes every respondent eligible for a voucher.

## Publication checklist

- The survey link works on a phone.
- The form contains the actual privacy notice and separate contact choices.
- Career changers can indicate their current coding experience.
- Topic, date and timetable are described as tentative where unresolved.
- Funding status matches the actual status.
- There is no guaranteed employment, admission or public-funding claim.
- A proposed pilot is not advertised as booked until its arrangements are confirmed.
- Statements about response counts and employer interest come from collected evidence.

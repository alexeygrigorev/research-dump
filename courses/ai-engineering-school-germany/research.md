# Research: an AI engineering education business in Germany

Updated: 2026-11-09.

This file preserves the desk research behind a proposed April cohort, with live online from Germany and in person in Berlin-Prenzlauer Berg near S+U Schönhauser Allee both in scope. Demand has **not** yet been validated: no survey responses, interviews, pilot outcomes or funded enrolments are reported here. Course duration, price, group size and timetable remain hypotheses.

## Current direction

The first iteration would be taught by Alexey personally, within the existing education and community business. Hiring is deferred until after the first cohort. April 2027 is the target month, conditional on demand, capacity, necessary approvals and participant funding.

The original desk research compared three outcomes. The current survey tests the first two:

1. **AI-assisted software engineering / agentic development:** using coding agents to develop and maintain software.
2. **AI application engineering:** building applications with LLMs, retrieval and agents.
3. **Programming foundations with AI:** acquiring the coding foundations needed for further technical study.

Beginners and career changers remain in scope for demand research. Higher support requirements are a delivery consideration, not a reason to exclude their responses. The initial preference for an audience that already codes is a workload hypothesis for a solo first cohort, not a conclusion about which market is largest.

The latest objective also includes identifying useful business expenditure that can legitimately be recognised in 2026. Accounting treatment must be checked separately; this research does not assume that spending, an invoice, a prepayment and a current-year deduction are interchangeable.

## Labour-market context

In its July 2026 release reporting **2025** outcomes, the Bundesagentur für Arbeit described a mixed IKT labour market:

- Registered vacancy stock fell by 22% year on year.
- The occupation-specific unemployment rate rose to 4.5%.
- Employment nevertheless increased by 2%.

These observations support investigating reskilling and upskilling needs. They do not establish that AI caused layoffs, that all software roles are shrinking, or that a particular short course will lead to employment. Source: [BA national IKT labour-market release](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/presse/2026-24-arbeitsmarkt-in-der-informations-und-kommunikationstechnik-ikt-im-spannungsfeld-konjunkturelle-schwaeche-trifft-auf-strukturellen-wandel).

For Berlin, BA reported 84,599 employees in IKT occupations in 2025, up 3.5%, alongside an annual average of 7,431 unemployed IKT professionals. Registered vacancies are only part of the hiring market and must not be treated as a complete inventory. Source: [BA Berlin-Brandenburg release](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/vor-ort/rd-bb/presse/2026-16-berlin-ist-der-fuhrende-standort-fur-ikt-beschaftigung-unterstutzung-von-zukunftsbranchen-macht-hoffnung).

### Small follow-up employer study

Before fixing the final syllabus, collect 20–30 relevant German job postings and speak with two to five hiring managers or technical leads. This is proposed work, not completed research.

Record the URL, date checked, open/closed status, location, level, language, real tasks, mandatory skills and evidence a candidate would need to present. Ask employers to review a proposed project or assessment, rather than seeking vague assurances that they would hire graduates.

## Segment the audience on independent dimensions

### Technical readiness

1. Builds and debugs applications with version control and tests.
2. Writes scripts, SQL or notebooks but has limited software-delivery experience.
3. Completes tutorials but struggles to build independently.
4. Has little or no coding experience.

### Situation and participation route

- Seeking another technical role, including after a layoff or contract end.
- Employed and updating skills.
- Moving from an adjacent technical role.
- Starting a transition from a nontechnical field.
- Considering employer funding, public funding or self-payment.

“Career changer” is not a technical level. A data analyst moving into software development and someone starting programming from scratch should not be counted as the same instructional audience.

A person's unemployment status does not establish funding eligibility. The selected provider and course must be approved, and individual funding conditions must be satisfied. An existing voucher can also have date, regional and learning-goal restrictions. Source: [BA Bildungsgutschein](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/karriere-und-weiterbildung/bildungsgutschein).

## Research method and interpretation

Use the [survey](materials/survey.en.md), [interviews and pilot](materials/interviews-and-pilot.md) and [outreach drafts](materials/outreach.md) to test demand before substantial expenditure.

Compare people who match the actual offer: topic, prerequisites, timing, daytime availability and a plausible payment or funding route. A response indicating interest in an evening course is valuable evidence of another need, but does not count as a commitment to four weekday mornings.

Self-pay willingness and public-funding readiness should be assessed separately. Someone who cannot personally pay may still be a suitable funded participant; the research must not adjudicate eligibility itself.

The proposed thresholds and survey sample sizes are management hypotheses. They are not observed conversion rates, certification requirements or proof that the programme will be profitable.

## Unresolved questions

- Which offer receives the strongest *matched* interest?
- Which applicants can demonstrate the prerequisites for a coherent first group?
- Do beginners want foundations, a longer career transition, or an existing product-building format?
- Can people commit to the proposed full-time schedule or a part-time schedule from April in their preferred format? For in-person learners, can they reliably reach Schönhauser Allee by 09:00?
- Is April too late for people seeking training now?
- What support would make this programme meaningfully useful alongside existing free materials?
- Can the founder allocate the full delivery workload while maintaining the existing business?
- Will the selected format, course classification, costs and continuity arrangements be accepted?
- Does the course make economic sense after accounting for founder time and, later, paid teaching support?

No conclusion about these questions should be manufactured from the size of an existing audience.

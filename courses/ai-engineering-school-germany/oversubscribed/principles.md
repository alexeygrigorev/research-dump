# Oversubscribed for the AI Engineering School: principles applied

**13 November 2026.** This file applies Daniel Priestley's *Oversubscribed* to the Bildungsgutschein school. It does not summarise the book again. The summary and its sources already exist:

- [Oversubscribed action checklist](../../../learning/book-action-checklists/oversubscribed.md): the eight principles, five campaign phases and the source guide. **This is the canonical summary.** Everything below refers to it by principle and phase number.
- [Product Shipping with AI: Oversubscribed launch plan](../../product-shipping/oversubscribed-launch-plan.md): the first application, for the Maven course starting in January.
- [Product Shipping marketing plan](../../product-shipping/marketing-plan.md): funnel, calendar and ready copy for that course.
- [Oversubscribed conference demand](../../../events/oversubscribed-conference-demand/): the same canon for a conference. Not used here.

What is new here is only what differs for a publicly funded, in-person school.

## What is different from the Maven course

| | Maven: Product Shipping with AI | AI Engineering School, Berlin |
|---|---|---|
| Who pays | The participant | Mostly the Agentur für Arbeit through a Bildungsgutschein, some self-pay |
| What the buyer gives up | Money | Three to four months of full-time attendance |
| What is scarce | 15 selected places, review time | About 15 seats in one room, one instructor's attention |
| Price as a filter | Yes, four routes | No. Price cannot select, so the application has to |
| Who else decides | Nobody | The Agentur decides each voucher. A certifier decides the course approval and the group size |
| Risk in the message | Overstating scarcity | Overstating funding, and readers assuming "funded" means "open to all" |
| Stage today | Enrollment | Announcement before approval: collect signals, not sales |

The main consequence: on Maven, price does part of the selection. Here the course costs the participant nothing, so demand can be large and unqualified. **Selection has to be explicit**, and the reason for the cap has to be stated, because nobody will infer it from a price.

## The eight principles, applied

Principle numbers follow the [checklist](../../../learning/book-action-checklists/oversubscribed.md#part-i--principles).

| # | Principle | Decision for the school |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Demand, capacity, profitability | Capacity is **about 15 seats**, an approximate number set by the room and by one instructor and still to be confirmed by the room specification and the AZAV group size. Demand is measured in applications, not in likes. Some people who could get funding will not get a seat, and we say so. See the [15-seat rationale](15-seat-rationale.md). |
| 2 | Your own audience | Not "everyone with a voucher". People in Germany who already write code, can study full-time in English in Berlin, and want an AI or software engineering job. They value Alexey's teaching and open courses more than the wider retraining market does. |
| 3 | Market before sale | The demand driver is **relationships and individual attention**: taught by the founder, every project reviewed. Not price (it is funded for most), not convenience (it is full-time and in person). The Zoomcamps, YouTube and newsletter are the education that comes before the sale. |
| 4 | Conditions for buying | Practical reason: a job. Personal outcome: a deployed project and the ability to explain it. Reason to act now: real application rounds and a real seat count. Proof: Zoomcamp graduates' results, with permission. No first-cohort testimonials exist yet, and none will be invented. |
| 5 | Differentiate, set your rules | The rules are public: application, practical task, full attendance, English, some programming first. We decline poor fits instead of filling seats. We state the limitation plainly: approval is pending and the cohort is about 15. |
| 6 | Ecosystem | Free Zoomcamps and YouTube → the school → AI Shipping Labs community → later, paid graduate teaching assistants. The small first step for a prospect is the application and the practical task, or a pilot session. |
| 7 | Customer's situation and language | The application form is the scorecard. Segment by coding level, availability and funding stage. Many readers are between jobs: speak to that privately in the form, never ask for it in public replies. A German short version exists because advisers and partners forward in German. |
| 8 | Experience worth recommending | The first 15 or so are the marketing for the second cohort. This is the business reason for the cap, and it is the same as the honest reason. |

## The five phases, as a calendar

Phase numbers follow the [checklist](../../../learning/book-action-checklists/oversubscribed.md#part-ii--the-campaign-driven-method). Dates reuse the existing [milestones](../schedule-and-capacity.md#milestones-through-launch). They are proposals.

| Phase | When | What happens | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Plan capacity, audience, delivery | Now to 27 Nov | Confirm the room capacity and the certifiable group size. Fix the selection criteria. Recheck founder hours at 15 learners. | One number we will not exceed. Published criteria. |
| 2. Build anticipation, gather signals | 13 Nov to mid-Dec | Announce the school and the 15 seats. Open the shortlist application. Run a pilot session. Organic content first, then small ad tests ([ads plan](paid-ads-channel-plan.md)). | Applications, completed tasks, pilot attendance. |
| 3. Measure demand, release deliberately | 27 Nov, then monthly rounds to 26 Feb | Review in announced rounds. Offer seats in batches, not all at once. Publish only true numbers. | Offers, accepted seats, waitlist. |
| 4. Sales and delivery follow-through | Jan to 19 Mar, then April to July | 20-minute conversations with a fixed structure. Help with the voucher conversation by giving accurate course facts. Then teach. | Seats with settled funding or payment. |
| 5. Celebrate, learn, improve | July 2027 onward | Collect outcomes with consent, debrief, open the next cohort to the waitlist first. | Honest cases for cohort two. |

## Demand signals, strongest last

This follows the Maven plan's [signal ladder](../../product-shipping/oversubscribed-launch-plan.md#what-counts-as-demand), with funding added. Record each person once, at their strongest signal.

1. Follows, likes, newsletter clicks: awareness.
2. Shortlist or application submitted: interest.
3. Practical task completed, pilot attended: applied engagement.
4. Conversation held and fit confirmed: qualified demand.
5. Seat offered and accepted: commitment, still subject to funding.
6. Voucher granted for this course, or self-pay contract signed: enrolled.

The book's planning multiples for 15 seats would be about 75 strong signals, 150 engaged ideal prospects or 1,500 weak ones. These are the author's heuristics, not conversion rates. The older [validation thresholds](../validation-plan.md) (15 matching prospects, eight concrete actions) were set for a group of eight and are too low to claim competition for 15. Level 6 depends on the Agentur, so plan for accepted applicants who do not get a voucher: that is what the waitlist is for.

## Rules we hold ourselves to

From the checklist's [safeguards](../../../learning/book-action-checklists/oversubscribed.md#interpretation-and-coverage), made specific:

- The cap is real. If the room or the certification allows fewer than 15, the lower number is published. A published cap is never raised for the same cohort.
- No invented counts, queues, countdowns or testimonials. "I expect more applications than seats" is allowed. "Oversubscribed" is used only when accepted applicants exceed seats.
- Deadlines are not moved to create a second last chance.
- Funding is never promised. The Agentur decides. Until the certificate exists, the course is "approval pending".
- Selection uses only the published criteria. Employment or benefit status is not one of them.
- Everyone who applies gets an answer.

## Open points this creates in the other AIES files

- **Group size.** Economics, validation thresholds and the AZAV notes assume eight participants and a certified group of 8 to 12. A cohort of about 15 needs a new costing, a new founder-hours estimate and a question to the certifier about maximum group size. Details in the [rationale](15-seat-rationale.md#what-has-to-be-checked).
- **Format.** "The room doesn't fit more" only applies in person. If the first cohort is online, attention is the only constraint and the copy changes ([positioning](positioning-and-messaging.md#if-the-cohort-is-online)).
- **Form.** There is still no application form, only the survey draft ([proposal](2026-11-13-announcement-proposal.md#3-the-form)).

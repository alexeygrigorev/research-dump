# Paid-ads channel plan, November and December 2026

**13 November 2026. A plan only.** No money has been spent, no ad account has been created or changed, and no campaign has been set up. All budgets are my rough planning figures, not quotes or benchmarks. Messaging follows [Oversubscribed](principles.md) and the [positioning](positioning-and-messaging.md).

## What the ads are for

In this phase the ads do one job: bring suitable people to the first-cohort application. In the book's terms this is phase 2, gathering signals. They do not sell a funded seat, because the course is not approved yet.

The unit that matters is a **suitable application**: someone who codes, can attend full-time in Berlin and can work in English. Clicks and impressions are not the goal.

## Who we are trying to reach

People in Germany, mostly Berlin and the surrounding area for an in-person cohort, who:

- already write some code (developers, QA, data people, technical career changers),
- are between jobs, in a notice period or expect to be,
- can study full-time in English,
- may be eligible for a Bildungsgutschein, which only the Agentur can decide.

Two other groups influence them: advisers at the Agentur für Arbeit and Jobcenter, who see course listings, and friends and former colleagues who forward posts.

## Order of work

### Stage 0: organic only, 13 to 30 November. Budget €0

1. Publish the [announcement](2026-11-13-announcement-proposal.md) on LinkedIn, Telegram, the newsletter and the community channels.
2. Put a pinned comment or description line under the most-watched relevant YouTube videos, and mention the school at the start of the next live stream.
3. One follow-up post about "funding is not admission".
4. Ask five to ten people in the Berlin network to forward the German text.
5. Record where each application came from (link parameter or the "how did you hear" question).

Stage 0 tells us which message gets suitable applications. Ads then amplify that message. Do not start paid tests before the application form, the privacy notice and a simple landing page exist: paid traffic to an unfinished form is wasted.

### Stage 1: small paid tests, 1 to 21 December. Budget about €1,500

| Channel | Test | Rough budget | What we learn |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Google Search | Germany, German and English keywords around Bildungsgutschein + KI/AI/software, and "AI bootcamp Berlin". Exact and phrase match, small set. | €500 | Whether intent traffic turns into suitable applications |
| YouTube | In-feed and skippable in-stream ad, a 30–45 second clip of Alexey in the room. Germany, tech and programming interests, plus a custom audience of people who watched the channel. | €400 | Whether video brings applicants or only views |
| Instagram and Facebook | One campaign, Berlin plus 50 km, two creatives (the room with 15 chairs; Alexey to camera). Feed and Reels. | €400 | Reach among people who don't follow tech channels |
| LinkedIn | Promote the best-performing organic post to Germany, software and data job titles. | €200 | Cost of reaching the exact professional audience |

Run each test for at least ten days with one message and one landing page, so the channels are comparable.

### Stage 2: scale what worked, late December to January. Budget €1,500–3,000, conditional

Only if stage 1 produced suitable applications. Put the money on the one or two channels with the lowest cost per suitable application. Drop the rest. Add retargeting of people who opened the form and did not finish.

If stage 1 produced clicks but no suitable applications, do not scale. Fix the page or the message first.

## Messages for ads

The same four messages as everywhere, shortened. Ad copy must be true before approval.

Headlines:

- "AI engineering school in Berlin. 15 seats. Apply."
- "Taught by Alexey Grigorev. First cohort April 2027. 15 seats."
- "KI-Weiterbildung in Berlin: 15 Plätze, Bewerbung erforderlich"

Body:

- "One room, one instructor, 15 seats. Full-time, in person, in English. Bildungsgutschein funding is planned; approval is pending. Funding doesn't get you a seat: you apply."
- "I teach this cohort myself and review every project. That works for about 15 people. First application round closes [date]."

Video script, about 30 seconds:

> This room has 15 seats. In April I start an AI engineering school here, and I teach it myself.
> I'm applying for approval so the Agentur für Arbeit can pay the tuition. But a voucher doesn't get you a seat.
> You apply, you do a short task, and I pick the cohort.
> The first round closes on [date]. The link is below.

Rules for ad copy:

- No "free", "kostenlos" or "100% gefördert" in any headline or image before approval.
- No countdown timers, no "only X left" unless X is the real number that day.
- Keyword ads may target "Bildungsgutschein", but the ad text must say approval is pending.
- Do not target or mention unemployment in the creative. Platforms restrict ads that imply knowledge of personal circumstances, and it is disrespectful.
- Check each platform's current rules for education and employment-related ads when setting up. I have not verified them for this plan.

## What to measure

| Measure | Why |
|---|---|
| Applications per channel | Basic volume |
| **Suitable applications per channel** | The only number used for decisions |
| Cost per suitable application | To compare channels |
| Completed practical tasks per channel | Quality after the form |
| Applicants who can attend in Berlin | Paid reach outside Berlin is useless for an in-person cohort |

Decision rule for the end of stage 1: keep a channel if it produced at least a few suitable applications at a cost Alexey would pay again; otherwise stop it. With budgets this small the numbers will be thin, so read the applications themselves, not only the totals.

Against the book's planning heuristics, 15 seats would want roughly 75 strong signals. Organic channels should supply most of those. Ads are a test of new channels, not the main source.

## After approval

These become possible only when the certificate exists, and they are probably worth more than any ad:

- A listing in KURSNET, the Agentur's own course database, which advisers use.
- Listings on private voucher portals.
- Ad copy that can say the course is approved for Bildungsgutschein.
- A one-page German fact sheet for advisers.

Plan the 2027 budget around these, using what stage 1 showed.

## Before any spend

- The application form and privacy notice are live.
- A landing page states 15 seats, the reason, the selection steps and the approval status.
- Source tracking works.
- Alexey has approved the budget and the copy.
- The seat number has been checked against the room and the certifier ([rationale](15-seat-rationale.md#what-has-to-be-checked)).

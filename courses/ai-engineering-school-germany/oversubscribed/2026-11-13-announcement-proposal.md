## 2. The announcement

### What to change, whatever the current text says

| If the current text says | Change it to | Why (Oversubscribed) |
|---|---|---|
| "I'm planning / exploring a bootcamp" as the headline | "I'm opening applications for the first cohort. 20 seats." | Lead with capacity. People value what is visibly limited. |
| "Take this five-minute survey" | "Apply for a seat" (same form, extended) | A survey asks for a favour. An application asks for commitment and gives a stronger signal. |
| "Funded by Bildungsgutschein" with nothing after it | "The Agentur für Arbeit can pay the tuition. It doesn't decide who gets a seat. I do, and there are 20." | Funding is not admission. Say it once, early. |
| No reason for the limit | "One room, one instructor. The room fits 20 and I can't give proper attention to more." | The limit must be a real, physical one. Invented scarcity is not allowed by the book or by us. |
| "Please share with anyone for whom it could be relevant" | "If you know one person in Germany who should be in this room, send them this." | Same ask, but about a specific person and a specific room. |
| Open-ended timing | A real first-round date | A reason to act now that is true. |
| Anything like "everyone welcome", "open to all", "sign up" | "Apply", "selected", "shortlist" | Admission language, not registration language. |

The self-pay price is the figure from the survey draft. The Bildungsgutschein notes discuss a €12,000–13,000 voucher price. Do not print a voucher price before it is approved.

### Questions to add

1. **What have you built or shipped with code?** One or two sentences and a link if you have one (GitHub, a deployed app, a work project you can describe). *Required.*
2. **How do you program today?** Daily at work / regularly on my own projects / I have completed courses but not built much / I am just starting. *Required.*
3. **Can you attend Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 16:00, in Berlin, from April to July 2027?** Yes / Yes, with these limits: [text] / No. *Required.*
4. **Why this cohort, and why now?** Three sentences at most. *Required.*
5. **What will you do with the skill in the six months after the course?** *Required.*
6. **If you aren't offered a seat in the first cohort, do you want a place on the waitlist for it and first notice of the next one?** Yes / No.
7. **How did you hear about this?** Optional, for channel tracking.

### Selection criteria to publish on the form

Publish what you will actually apply, and apply only what you published.

- Programming experience that fits the track.
- Can attend the full schedule in the chosen format.
- Can work in English.
- A concrete goal for after the course.
- Completed the practical task.

Do not select on employment status, benefit status, age, nationality or anything else unrelated to learning in this cohort. Funding stage is a planning fact, not a ranking criterion.

### Waitlist rules to publish

- 20 seats. When they are offered and accepted, the cohort is full.
- An offered seat is held for [7 days]. After that it goes to the next person on the waitlist.
- The waitlist is ordered by the round in which an applicant was accepted, then by application date.
- A seat is only final once funding or payment is settled. If someone's voucher is not granted, they can choose self-pay or release the seat.
- Waitlisted applicants hear about the next cohort before it is announced publicly.

### New thank-you screen

> Thank you. Your application for the first cohort is in.
>
> I read every application myself. You'll hear from me by [date] with one of three answers: an invitation to the practical task, a place on the waitlist, or a no with a suggestion for what to do first.
>
> An application is not a seat and not a funding decision. The Agentur für Arbeit decides on the Bildungsgutschein, and the course approval is still in progress.

The privacy notice and contact method are still placeholders in the survey draft. The form cannot go live without them, and an application collects more personal data than the survey did.

## 5. Do not publish

- "Free", "kostenlos", "100% funded", "guaranteed funding" — approval is pending and funding is individual.
- "Only 3 seats left", "filling fast", any application count — until the numbers exist.
- "AZAV-certified", "zertifiziert" — until the certificate exists.
- A job guarantee or a placement rate.
- "20 seats" if the room or the certified group size is smaller.
- An application link that points to the unchanged research survey.

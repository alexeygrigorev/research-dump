# Superseded scenario: a staffed, full-time AI Engineering programme

Archive prepared: 2026-11-09.

**This is a historical planning scenario, not the current first-iteration plan.** It assumed an experienced lead instructor and coordination support from the first cohort, 560 synchronous UE and a provisional €8,900 tuition. The user subsequently chose to teach the first iteration personally, validate demand before substantial expenditure and keep beginners in the research.

The model is retained for future comparison if a staffed programme is reconsidered. None of its budgets are supplier quotes, committed expenditure, actual revenue, personal available funds or approved public-funding prices.

## Original operating hypothesis

| Item | Historical assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Product | AI Engineering for learners with Python and software/data experience |
| Delivery | Live online; English teaching with German administrative information |
| Volume | Fourteen teaching weeks, 40 UE weekly, 560 synchronous UE total |
| Live contact | 420 clock hours total; 30 hours weekly |
| Provisional price | €8,900 per participant, subject to classification, cost review and VAT treatment |
| Cohort | Target ten, planned capacity twelve; eight only as a deliberate learning investment |
| Main instructor | Experienced lead, with documented qualified continuity arrangements |
| Founder | Four live teaching hours plus four preparation, quality and management hours per week |
| Operations | Paid coordination support |
| Initial delivery target | April 2027, conditional on approvals and enrolments |

The later solo hypothesis of 192 UE is a different product and workload. It cannot inherit this scenario's price or revenue forecast.

These allocations were pedagogical hypotheses, not an approved syllabus. An appropriate duration must follow the learning goal and audience. [SGB III §179](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__179.html) requires an appropriate duration and cost; it does not justify extending a course to reach a preferred total price.

## Staff and delivery assumptions

- Lead instructor: 26 live hours and eight preparation/review hours weekly, 34 hours total.
- Founder: four live hours and four additional hours weekly, eight hours total.
- Coordinator during delivery: eight hours weekly.
- Qualified backup: genuine availability, access to teaching materials and a rehearsed continuity process.
- Paid trials: verify subject competence, teaching and troubleshooting before relying on a person for a full cohort.

The eight-hour preparation allowance for the lead was an estimate to test. It was not evidence that every instructor could deliver the programme within that time.

The staffing model still needed appropriate employment/contract classification and complete costs. A contractor label alone would not resolve employment-status questions. No person was hired under this scenario.

Founder time was valued at 14 × eight hours × €100 = €11,200 for management comparison. It was not a payment of salary to oneself, and preparation before the cohort was not included in that time estimate. These results are before personal taxes and do not predict actual profit.

### Sensitivity checks

- At **€7,500 tuition** and ten participants, the model gives approximately **−€2,138** after the same delivery costs, setup and founder-time value.
- Increasing the lead's full cost from **€60 to €80 per hour** adds **€10,472**, including the 10% contingency. The ten-person result falls to approximately **€1,390**.
- If €8,900 were a VAT-inclusive customer price at 19%, retained net revenue would be approximately **€7,478.99 per participant**, not €8,900. Input-VAT treatment would also need recalculation. No educational VAT exemption was assumed to be automatic.

These sensitivities explain why removing premises does not by itself determine an online course's price or profitability.

## Price approval is separate from the business model

The [BA B-DKS table effective from 1 July 2026](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/datei/tabelle-der-kosten-fuer-massnahmen-der-beruflichen-weiterbildung-2024_ba049411.pdf) lists €13.24 per UE for Softwareentwicklung—Spezialist and €15.46 for Softwareentwicklung—Experte.

At 560 UE, €8,900 corresponds to approximately **€15.89 per UE**. That is about 20% above the specialist reference and 2.8% above the expert reference. The correct category follows the actual course; it is not selected for a convenient price.

An excess needs justified necessary expenditure. An excess above 25% additionally needs BA agreement under [SGB III §179](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__179.html). Being below that threshold is not automatic approval. The official costing framework normally assumes twelve participants for a group measure, with a lower justified group size requiring appropriate treatment; see [AZAV §3](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/azav/__3.html).

This internal cost model was not the formal FKS calculation. Setup allocation, group size, reasonable costs and any margin would need to be handled in that calculation.

## Liquidity hypothesis

The scenario paired €18,000 of preparation with approximately **€20,000 of delivery liquidity**, a total modelled requirement of €38,000 separate from tax obligations.

This was not a guaranteed eight-week runway. Eight weeks of only the lead and coordinator would cost **€18,560** under the stated rates, before other expenses. A stress case of **€23,000–25,000** liquidity would require reduced setup spending or additional financing.

BA payment timing was a reason to separate contracted revenue from cash available at the beginning. The researched [BA measure questionnaire](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/datei/fragebogen-massnahme_ba025870.pdf) describes monthly payment arrangements in arrears. Actual billing, attendance, withdrawals and payment conditions must be confirmed for the measure.

## Historical delegation path

The old scenario envisaged an experienced lead from the outset, then paid graduate assistants, then more founder-independent delivery after demonstrated quality. Moving the second cohort offline or changing the legal entity was a separate future decision.

Useful elements survive in the current plan:

- Document each lesson and assessment in a teacher pack.
- Select graduate assistants by technical and teaching ability.
- Pay for defined work and assess performance.
- Verify continuity and quality before reducing founder involvement.
- Recalculate economics with real paid teaching costs.

The assumption of hiring a lead before the first cohort does **not** survive. The current first iteration is founder-led and begins with demand validation. See [current curriculum options](../curriculum-options.md).

## Reasons this scenario was superseded

1. The user wants to conduct the first iteration personally.
2. Demand must be assessed before substantial investment.
3. Agentic software development should be tested alongside AI application engineering.
4. Beginners and career changers remain a research segment, with their support needs measured separately.
5. A smaller live teaching volume requires new pricing and capacity analysis.
6. The current year-end expense objective requires a separate, accounting-method-specific analysis; historical budget timing is not tax advice.

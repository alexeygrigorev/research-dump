## The capacity constraint

The April cohort is planned as full-time, with live instruction and supervised practice. At 30 synchronous clock hours plus 5–7 hours of preparation and assessment and 3–4 hours of administration and learner support, budget **38–41 active founder hours per teaching week**. This is a planning estimate. For the calendar, reserve the full 35-hour teaching week plus 8–11 hours outside class. Add travel and room setup for Berlin. ### Choosing the April format

1. Compare matching candidates per topic and format. A match can start in April, meets the prerequisite and language expectations, and can commit to the tested schedule. Track actions completed and funding progress.
2. Use the existing research targets: 15 matching prospects, eight completed next actions, six pilot attendees and four who can explain the exercise. Apply pilot evidence to the format actually tested. If Berlin leads after an online pilot, use a local walkthrough or small practical session to test the trip and room.
3. Compare the [cost model](economics.md), approval timeline and founder capacity. For Berlin, include travel, opening, setup and close-down on top of the full teaching calendar and the support and preparation budget.
4. If both formats are viable, use committed candidate preference and delivery margin to decide which to launch first. Confirm with the FKS how to add the second format later.

### Berlin room checklist

- **Availability.** Check the full calendar of 60 teaching days, setup and close-down access, rehearsal and audit access, make-up slots and concurrent uses.
- **Terms.** Obtain the exact address, written rent and inclusions, right to use the room for teaching, deposit, insurance responsibilities, cancellation dates and any conditions tied to approvals or enrolment.
- **Facilities.** Check usable capacity including the instructor, desks, lighting, ventilation, toilets, accessibility, evacuation route, Wi-Fi under load, power, display and guest access.
- **Evidence.** Gather the floor plan and room specifications. Confirm with the FKS when it needs written permission or a contract and which site checks it will require. [AZAV §5](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/azav/__5.html) · [Beirat recommendations](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/datei/dok_ba014848.pdf).

## Graduate teaching assistants and later iterations

| Stage | Graduate role | Alexey's responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| First cohort | Student work and an optional expression of future interest | Teaching, assessment, support and administration |
| After graduation | Paid trial task or teach-back and role preparation | Select for technical independence, explanation and dependable feedback |
| Next cohort | Environment setup, common questions, initial rubric-based review, supervised explanations | Core curriculum, difficult cases and final quality decisions |
| Later cohorts | More teaching only after competence and the operating model are demonstrated | Programme management and outcome review |

Do not assume that current students can cover the founder's illness, work for free, or become lead teachers merely by graduating. Do not promise all students a job. Model the second cohort with a **paid TA**, and the later delegated model with a **fully costed lead instructor**.

A GmbH and delegated teaching are later decisions. An Einzelunternehmen can be an employer, and a new legal entity may require changes to certification and contracts. Use first-cohort evidence to decide when to add the second delivery format and when to delegate teaching.

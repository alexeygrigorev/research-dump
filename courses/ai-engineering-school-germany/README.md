# AI engineering school in Germany

**Working research and launch plan · updated 12 November 2026.** Owner: Alexey Grigorev. Target: a first cohort in April 2027, with two formats in scope: live online from Germany and in person in Berlin (Prenzlauer Berg, borough of Pankow, near S+U Schönhauser Allee), potentially funded through Bildungsgutschein, developed alongside the existing education and community business.

## Current direction

The offering is an intensive, multi-month bootcamp in AI and software development, with live instruction and hands-on project work. The current scheduling hypothesis is 12 teaching weeks.

The first iteration is to be taught and run by Alexey himself. **No employee or teaching-assistant hire is planned now.** First use the existing network to compare demand for two tracks:

1. **AI-assisted software engineering / agentic development:** people who already code and want to use coding agents to change, test and ship software.
2. **AI application engineering:** people who already code and want to build reliable applications with LLMs, retrieval and tools.

Both tracks assume some programming experience. Career changers are welcome, and the survey’s open response can capture other learning needs.

The latest priority is to identify useful project expenditure that can lawfully reduce **2026** taxable profit. **The accounting method is confirmed as Bilanzierung (9 November 2026).** The founder wishes to prepay genuine school-startup costs in 2026 and, if legally possible, recognize them in the **2026 tax result**. Under Bilanzierung the payment date alone is normally insufficient: prepayments for unperformed 2027 services are generally assets or prepaid expenses, not 2026 operating expense. Work performed and economically attributable to 2026 can ordinarily be booked in 2026 even if invoiced or paid in 2027, subject to asset/capitalization rules. §5(5) EStG has a narrow waiver for qualifying small transitory prepaid amounts (generally at or below €800), and §7g EStG offers a separate potential 2026 deduction for planned qualifying movable depreciable business assets—not services or shares. Use the [2026 expense plan](2026-expense-plan.md) for payment decisions.

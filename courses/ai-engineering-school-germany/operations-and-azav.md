# Operations and the AZAV route

**Planning snapshot: 9 November 2026.** The current model is an existing Berlin Einzelunternehmen, a first cohort taught by Alexey, and live online delivery. No employee is to be hired for this iteration. The provisional course has 192 synchronous Unterrichtseinheiten (UE), with additional independent project work. These are design hypotheses, not an approved measure.

This file records the operational work still needed. It does not claim that certification, individual funding, a course price, a classroom, or a launch date has been approved. See [curriculum options](curriculum-options.md), [economics](economics.md), [schedule and capacity](schedule-and-capacity.md), and the [German FKS inquiry](materials/fks-inquiry.de.md).

## 1. Three separate decisions

| Decision | What it establishes | Evidence to retain |
|---|---|---|
| Trägerzulassung | The provider satisfies the organizational requirements | Provider certificate, scope, locations, conditions and surveillance dates |
| Maßnahmezulassung | The specific programme satisfies the course requirements | Course certificate and approved concept, hours, price and delivery conditions |
| Individual funding | A particular participant may use funding for this course | The relevant individual funding documents, checked against dates, educational goal and other restrictions |

The Bundesagentur für Arbeit states that both the provider and the selected course need approval by a Fachkundige Stelle (FKS). A Bildungsgutschein is normally restricted by time, region and educational goal. Survey interest, unemployment or possession of an unrelated voucher does not establish funding for April 2027. Since 2025, the Agentur für Arbeit also handles this financial support for Jobcenter clients. [BA: Bildungsgutschein](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/karriere-und-weiterbildung/bildungsgutschein).

The certificates specified in SGB III §181 concern approval of the provider and measure; they do not establish a new state-recognized professional qualification for the graduate. Describe the proposed outcome as professional continuing education with a provider-issued completion certificate and demonstrable project work, unless a separate recognized qualification is actually involved. [SGB III §181](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__181.html).

## 2. What does not have to be purchased immediately

Use the existing company, content, teaching platform and community for demand research. The survey and interviews can establish which programme is worth preparing. A short interactive research workshop should be described accurately; making a vocational distance course free is not a general exemption from FernUSG.

A GmbH, a permanent classroom and a full-time coordinator are not assumed launch purchases. AZAV expressly accommodates natural persons as providers; the application still needs an appropriate organization, financial reliability and evidence for the actual delivery model. Confirm the existing business registration and tax classification with the adviser. For a home administrative office, ask the FKS to confirm the address, access and audit arrangement before assuming it is acceptable. [AZAV §2](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/azav/__2.html).

For an entirely online first cohort, the budget does not include classroom rent. Document instead the actual learning environment: video platform, LMS, equipment requirements, participant access, technical support, fallback connectivity and access control. Decide how participants without suitable hardware will be supported before advertising a complete package; do not reveal unexpected mandatory equipment or software purchases after enrolment. Suitability of the spatial, staffing and technical arrangements is part of course approval. [SGB III §179](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__179.html).

## 3. A small working quality system

The legal requirement is to apply a quality assurance system. It is not enough to own a template pack, and the cited legislation does not prescribe buying a separate ISO 9001 certificate. The provider also needs capable teaching personnel, support for labour-market integration and appropriate participant contract terms. [SGB III §178](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__178.html).

The following is a proposed small-team implementation, not a legally prescribed folder count. Alexey can create it himself from existing materials. Keep participant records and contracts in a private operational system; this public repository should contain only blank templates or anonymized summaries.

| Working artifact | Minimum useful contents | How to demonstrate it works |
|---|---|---|
| Organization and responsibilities | Business details, administrative address, responsible person, contact routes, course responsibilities | One page showing who does what, including when the founder is unavailable |
| Financial plan | Setup costs, ongoing costs, cash reserve, payment timing, minimum viable cohort | A monthly cash forecast and explicit launch decision |
| Labour-market evidence | Target roles, dated vacancy examples, employer interviews, programme mapping | Explain which workplace tasks each module addresses; distinguish evidence from assumptions |
| Course specification | Audience, prerequisites, outcomes, modules, synchronous UE, independent hours, calendar | One internally consistent programme and timetable |
| Teaching competence | CV, professional projects, existing course examples, teaching references and relevant training | A short teaching demonstration and examples of learner feedback, with appropriate permissions |
| Admission | Questionnaire, prerequisite task, rubric, suitability decision and participant terms | Assess a sample task using the rubric and explain the decision |
| Daily delivery | Attendance, covered topics, exercises, learning progress and incidents | A filled example from a rehearsal, explicitly labelled as a rehearsal |
| Continuity | Illness, technical failure, communication, make-up teaching and any agreed substitute arrangement | Walk through a missed lesson and a longer absence; record gaps and corrections |
| Feedback and improvement | Feedback form, complaint route, response targets, improvement register | Log a test complaint and the resulting change |
| Infrastructure and data | Platform access, backups, recording policy, privacy information, retention rules | Test a learner login, restoration and access removal |
| Completion and career support | Assessment, project defence, certificate wording, portfolio/CV support and outcome follow-up | Grade a sample project and test the follow-up process |
| Internal review | Version list, periodic review, deficiencies, corrective actions and owner | A dated review with concrete improvements, rather than an empty template |

A new provider should show how the requirements will be met instead of inventing a history of funded graduates. Existing teaching experience can support the application; ask the FKS which evidence it will accept. The quality system can be proportionate to the operation, but it must be usable. [AZAV §2](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/azav/__2.html).

**Proposed December deliverable:** course specification, admission rubric, attendance template, one graded rehearsal exercise, tested technical access, an incident/complaint example, an illness walkthrough and a short internal review. This produces reviewable evidence without purchasing an elaborate consultancy package.

## 4. If the sole instructor becomes ill

The rules reviewed do not establish a universal minimum number of employed teachers. The Beirat recommendations for Fachbereich 4 do, however, explicitly require documentation of **Vertretungsregelungen** as part of teaching organization. An acceptable solo arrangement therefore needs a written FKS answer. [Beirat recommendations, 17 June 2026, p. 34](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/datei/dok_ba014848.pdf).

Propose and test the following operational scheme; do not describe it as already accepted:

| Event | Proposed procedure to discuss with the FKS | Evidence to prepare |
|---|---|---|
| One missed morning | Notify participants promptly, record the cancellation, offer a specific make-up slot and preserve the approved learning outcomes/hours | Message template, make-up policy and realistic reserve slots |
| Several days of absence | Escalation point, revised timetable, participant communication and FKS/BA notifications as required | Written escalation rules and responsibility for communication |
| Longer absence | Qualified one-off cover if required and available, or an agreed interruption/rescheduling route | Actual availability and competence evidence for any substitute, plus access to teaching materials |
| Internet or platform failure | Alternative connection/platform, incident log and make-up plan for lost teaching | A completed technical rehearsal |

Recordings do not automatically replace missed synchronous teaching. Students from the first cohort are not a qualified substitute on day one. A backup contact who has never agreed to be available is not a continuity arrangement.

No permanent hire is proposed now. If the FKS requires a qualified external substitute, determine the smallest credible contractual arrangement and its cost before commissioning the full certification process. If a workable solo arrangement cannot be approved, postpone the funded launch or change its organization; do not assume the requirement disappears.

## 5. Teaching hours, projects and shared community events

The working model is 48 mornings × 4 UE = **192 synchronous UE**, equivalent to **144 clock hours**. Independent work is currently estimated at about eight clock hours per teaching week and shown separately.

The BA implementation note fixes a teaching UE at 45 minutes and asynchronous components at 60-minute hours. Relevant asynchronous costs may enter the overall calculation, but the denominator for the cost per teaching UE is the number of **synchronous** UE. An exercise completed alone cannot simply be relabelled a funded live lesson. [BA Umsetzungshinweis 1/2026](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/datei/umsetzungshinweis-01-2026_ba055638.pdf).

For the first iteration, shared AI Shipping Labs events are proposed as optional extracurricular activities outside the 192 mandatory UE. Reusing preparation and examples is useful. If shared events later become mandatory teaching, agree their educational purpose, access, instructor supervision, attendance and cost treatment with the FKS. The founder cannot simultaneously host a podcast and teach the same scheduled live course hours.

## 6. ZFU/FernUSG review is a separate task

Assess the exact mix of live interaction, recordings, independent work, assignments, feedback and contractual rights. ZFU distinguishes interactive synchronous teaching from asynchronous study; recordings that make attendance unnecessary can affect the assessment. The proposed independent project hours mean that the complete design must be reviewed, rather than relying on the word “live”. Some free vocational distance courses can also be covered. The ZFU FAQ states that the reform proposal has not yet changed the applicable law. [ZFU FAQ](https://zfu.de/veranstaltende/faq).

Before contracts or a regulated course offer, obtain a written classification and, if needed, pursue ZFU approval. Coordinate this with the FKS; AZAV approval does not itself resolve the FernUSG question. Recheck the law before launch. The output should be a decision on the actual programme, its permitted marketing/contract sequence, additional cost and lead time—not a generic statement about all online courses.

## 7. Cost approval and group size

There is no single maximum euro price for every Bildungsgutschein course. The FKS checks the educational classification, necessary costs and appropriate duration. Costs above the applicable B-DKS require justification through necessary special expenditure; more than 25% above it additionally requires BA agreement. Do not select a higher occupational level solely to obtain its rate. [SGB III §179](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__179.html).

The standard group-cost calculation under AZAV starts from **12 participants**. The business planning hypothesis of eight actual students does not silently override that basis. Ask the FKS how the concept and calculation should document the smaller first group and any permitted departure. Eight is a management threshold in this plan, not a statutory minimum. [AZAV §3](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/azav/__3.html).

Get a complete written FKS quote: provider approval, one course, audit, surveillance, corrections, changes, travel, VAT, payment milestones and possible additional cost-approval work. Keep the 192-UE economics separate from the [historical staffed 560-UE scenario](archive/staffed-scenario.md). The tax treatment of any genuine 2026 prepayments is addressed in the [expense plan](2026-expense-plan.md).

## 8. From approval to a funded participant

1. Finalize and obtain the provider and course approvals for the actual legal entity and delivery model.
2. Prepare accurate participant information and contracts, including the applicable funding-related withdrawal terms.
3. Check each prospective participant's suitability and individual funding route. A November expression of interest is not an April funding commitment.
4. Coordinate the BA registration of the approved course instance and the participant documents with the responsible Maßnahmeagentur.
5. Confirm the technology, schedule and learning requirements with each enrolled participant before the start.
6. Run attendance, progress, changes and billing records from the first day.

The BA instructions say to submit the Kurzfragebogen when the first redeemable voucher (or relevant employee registration) exists, preferably two weeks before the start, with the required certificates and annexes. Follow the current sequence for the voucher copies and the Maßnahmebogen. Provider IDs, course certificate numbers, a BA Maßnahmennummer and a public catalogue entry have different functions. [BA completion instructions](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/datei/dok_ba037649.pdf).

KURSNET offers a free publication route for eligible vocational education offers. A catalogue listing is useful for discovery; it does not constitute course approval or guarantee payment. Confirm the current route for display in the BA search services when the programme is ready. [BA: Kurse anbieten](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/institutionen/kurse-anbieten).

For direct payment, the BA form specifies monthly instalments paid **in arrears**, with the first instalment due after one month has elapsed and transfer following the relevant payment date. It also includes conditions for withdrawal, non-starts, early departures and overpayments. Model sufficient cash to operate before receipts arrive; do not treat all tuition as cash received on day one. [BA Kurzfragebogen, June 2026, p. 5](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/datei/fragebogen-massnahme_ba025870.pdf).

## 9. Later changes and the partner option

Hiring a TA does not by itself require a GmbH. Before graduates teach, assess technical ability and teaching skill, define a paid role, provide supervision and check employment/tax treatment. Update the approved staffing and quality documents as required. Graduation alone is not evidence of lead-instructor competence.

If a GmbH, in-person location or different programme is introduced later, check the effect on approvals, contracts and payment arrangements before implementation. Do not assume an approval follows the founder automatically to a new legal entity. Applications and approvals are provider-, measure- and potentially location-specific. [SGB III §181](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__181.html).

A partnership with an existing provider deserves separate research if the own-provider route is too slow. It is not simply a certificate rental: the Beirat rule limits ordinary subcontracting to an unapproved provider in Fachbereich 4 to a minor portion, generally at most 10%, while the approved provider remains responsible. The contractual and staffing model must be assessed with the partner's FKS. [Beirat recommendations, p. 33](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/datei/dok_ba014848.pdf).

## 10. Launch decision

Before confirming April, obtain evidence that the following are true:

- The actual programme and provider have the necessary approvals, and the ZFU question has a documented answer.
- The price, VAT treatment, course hours and participant conditions are consistent across the contract, certificate, marketing and cash forecast.
- The illness arrangement is accepted and can actually be used.
- The founder has freed the required teaching, preparation, assessment and administration hours.
- Enough suitable participants have real paid or funded places under the approved model.
- The registration and participant paperwork can be completed in time, and liquidity covers delayed receipts.

These are proposed management gates. Approval bodies have not committed to the project's internal dates. A failed gate should produce a documented remedy or a revised start date.

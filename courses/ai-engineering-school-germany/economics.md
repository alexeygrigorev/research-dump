# Pricing, workload and cash economics

**Model date: 9 November 2026.** All proposed tuition, enrolment counts and cost envelopes are assumptions. No FKS quote, tuition approval or financing decision has been obtained. The historical 560-UE model is [archived separately](archive/staffed-scenario.md).

## What changed when the first course became solo

The earlier staffed scenario assumed 560 synchronous UE and a provisional €8,900 fee. The full-time scenario has **480 synchronous UE**, or **360 live clock hours**, including supervised project work. The AZAV measure fee needs a separate calculation. Online delivery may avoid classroom rent, but “online” itself neither sets the price nor establishes a universal discount.

For comparison, the researched public pages advertised Ironhack remote AI at about €8,500 and WBS Data Science at about €9,900. These were advertised self-pay observations for different programmes, not verified approved Bildungsgutschein fees for an equivalent programme. See the [market comparison](research.md) for limitations. No independent confirmation of a directly comparable €13,000–14,000 funded offer was established in this research.

## B-DKS references, not an automatic maximum

The [BA table effective 1 July 2026](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/datei/tabelle-der-kosten-fuer-massnahmen-der-beruflichen-weiterbildung-2024_ba049411.pdf) contains the following example professional categories. The URL contains “2024”; the inspected document is the July 2026 version. Recheck the applicable version and category before submitting a course.

| Category | Reference per synchronous UE | 480 UE per participant | Eight participants at that arithmetic reference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 43413 Softwareentwicklung, Spezialist | €13.24 | €6,355.20 | €50,841.60 |
| 43414 Softwareentwicklung, Experte | €15.46 | €7,420.80 | €59,366.40 |
| 43423 Programmierung, Spezialist | €13.25 | €6,360.00 | €50,880.00 |

These multiplications do not constitute a certified fee. Choose the occupational level from the actual learning objective and audience, not the higher rate. The FKS reviews the cost calculation and justified necessary expenditure. Costs above the applicable average need justification; exceeding it by **more than 25%** additionally requires BA agreement. See [§179 SGB III](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__179.html) and [§3 AZAV](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/azav/__3.html).

For orientation, €9,000 divided by 480 UE is **€18.75 per UE**. This uses the public self-pay price purely for arithmetic comparison. The AZAV costing uses the actual eligible costs and synchronous teaching units. The [BA implementation note](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/datei/umsetzungshinweis-01-2026_ba055638.pdf) distinguishes synchronous teaching units from asynchronous learning; necessary costs of independent learning may still form part of the calculation.

AZAV costing generally uses a twelve-participant group assumption. A first actual cohort of eight is a separate management choice that must be reconciled with the submitted concept and costing, not treated as an exemption from the rules.

## Solo cohort illustration

Assume a retained tuition amount of **€9,000 per participant** and eight actual participants:

| Item | Illustration |
|---|---:|
| Revenue before external expenses | €72,000 |
| Active founder effort at 38–41 h × 12 equivalent teaching weeks | 456–492 h |
| Founder time at an assumed €100/h opportunity cost | €45,600–49,200 |
| Economic result before external expenses and preparation before launch | **€22,800–26,400** |

The €100/h rate is a comparison assumption, not Alexey's reported pay rate, tax deduction or salary to himself. The founder's own time is not an ordinary deductible payment of an Einzelunternehmen. The delivery span includes public-holiday effects; administrative effort may add to the simple twelve-week multiplication.

Subtract actual external costs: certification and surveillance, legal review, software/cloud usage, required learner provision, marketing and other administration. Founder pre-launch development is additional time. A first cohort may still be a deliberate product-validation investment, but cash remaining in the bank does not by itself demonstrate a profitable school.

VAT must be resolved before publishing a final tuition price. The table assumes the business retains the full €9,000 before expenses. If that amount includes VAT that must be remitted, retained revenue is lower. Education exemption and input-VAT treatment are separate questions; see [§4 UStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__4.html), [§15 UStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__15.html) and the [expense plan](2026-expense-plan.md).

## Spending gates

| Stage | Proposed financial rule | Required output |
|---|---|---|
| November research | €0–300 cap using existing tools | Segmented demand and one practical test |
| Initial scope clarification | Obtain written quotes first; a bounded advisory task might have a €500–1,500 envelope | Written answers to a specific legal, format or accounting question |
| After positive demand and capacity decisions | Consider an optional €5,000–10,000 total year-end envelope, subject to actual quotes | Useful preparation, legal documents, certification services or genuine agreed advances |
| Beyond that envelope | New decision from the full costing and remaining uncertainty | Clear incremental value; enough cash left for taxes and operations |

These amounts are management limits, **not researched supplier prices**. The €5,000–10,000 range is not a certification quote, a minimum investment or authority to place orders. It includes earlier preparatory spending when calculating the total. There is no requirement to exhaust it, and no assumption that €30,000–40,000 needs to be spent.

The existing example models live online delivery. Build a second budget for the Schönhauser Allee room using written rental terms. Include 60 teaching days plus setup, any make-up days, access, cleaning, equipment, insurance and founder travel. Track refundable deposit separately as cash tied up and model the cancellation exposure. A new LMS, studio and permanent hire are outside the base model. Existing tools and self-authored documents should be reused where adequate. Purchase external work for concrete gaps that remain after that review.

Use the same tuition and enrolment assumptions to compare the two formats first, then update them with actual quotes and FKS feedback. Compare 6, 8 and 10 paid or funded places as business sensitivities. For each case calculate revenue retained after applicable VAT, contribution after costs, founder hours and the lowest monthly cash balance. Calculate the viable seat count for each format from the actual cost and net revenue per learner. The AZAV calculation has its own rules for group size.

## Liquidity and enrolment risk

Voucher funding is not equivalent to receiving the whole course fee before the first lesson. The [BA measure questionnaire](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/datei/fragebogen-massnahme_ba025870.pdf) describes monthly payments after the relevant period. Model the actual authorised payment schedule, possible processing delays, withdrawals and the terms of any self-pay contracts.

Prepare a monthly cash view from November through the end of the cohort:

- Opening project cash and separate tax reserve.
- Actual service payments and genuine prepayments, with their tax classification.
- Recurring operating payments, annual surveillance and possible substitute-teacher costs.
- Receipts based on payment timing, not the nominal value of vouchers or a waitlist.
- A delayed-launch case and a smaller-cohort case.

Cash moved into a reserve is still useful liquidity but is not itself a deductible business expense. Tax relief should be modelled separately from gross cash paid and from VAT recovery. Paying a cost in 2026 may accelerate a deduction; it does not allow the same expense to be deducted again in 2027.

## Conditions for a repeatable school

Before cohort two, price the actual support workload and a paid graduate TA. Before transferring the core teaching, price a qualified lead instructor's teaching, preparation, feedback and absence coverage. Include recruitment and oversight time. The goal of eventually managing rather than teaching requires this model to work with paid labour.

Review three outcomes after the first cohort: learner results, total founder hours and contribution after all relevant costs. Only then decide whether to repeat the first format, add the second format, change the fee or duration, or create a separate company. More live hours should be justified by learning needs, never added solely to reach a desired funded price.
